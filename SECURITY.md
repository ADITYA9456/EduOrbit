# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately. Do not open a public issue.

- **Email:** [SECURITY CONTACT EMAIL, TO BE FILLED BY THE OWNER BEFORE PUBLISHING]
- **GitHub:** use "Report a vulnerability" under the repository's Security tab (the owner must enable private vulnerability reporting in the repository settings).

Include what you found, where, the steps to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. Do not include other people's personal data in your report.

## What to expect (proposed targets, the owner should confirm they can keep them)

We aim to acknowledge within 2 working days, give a first assessment within 7 days, and keep you informed until it is fixed. We will credit you if you want credit.

## Scope

In scope: this repository's application code, its Supabase database policies and migrations, and the deployed EduOrbit website.

Out of scope: denial of service and volume testing, social engineering, physical attacks, findings that need a rooted device or a compromised browser, and third-party services (Supabase, Vercel, Google, YouTube, Judge0 hosts) which have their own programs.

## Please do not

Access, change or delete data that is not yours, run automated scanners against the production site at high volume, submit code to the judge that tries to harm the host, or disclose the issue publicly before it is fixed.

Good-faith research that follows these rules will not be pursued by us. This is a statement of intent, not a legal safe-harbour guarantee.

## Supported versions

Only the current `main` branch and the live deployment are supported.
